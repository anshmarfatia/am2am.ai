FAQ

Straight answers.

Will my team have to learn something new?

No. They keep using the chat tool they already use. The agent works inside the threads they're already in.

What if the agent makes a mistake?

Every write to ERPNext is logged and reversible. You'll see exactly what changed in the audit trail, and you can roll it back.

What chat platforms work?

WhatsApp Business, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Rocket.Chat. Others on request.

How long does setup take?

Two to three weeks from signed agreement to live on your team's threads.

Where does my data go?

It stays in your ERPNext instance, on hosting you already control. The agent reads and writes there — it does not copy your project data into a separate store of ours.
